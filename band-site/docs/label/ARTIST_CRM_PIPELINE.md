# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Artist CRM Pipeline

Purpose: define the internal artist pipeline from first discovery through release or archive.

Use one stage per artist at a time. Keep owner notes factual and current.

## Lead Found

- What it means: An artist or band has been discovered but not contacted.
- Next action: Review public music and decide whether outreach is worth sending.
- Email/template to use: `OUTREACH_MESSAGES.md` direct message or email template.
- Red flags: No accessible songs, unclear artist identity, fake-looking numbers.
- Owner notes:

## Contacted

- What it means: KAMDRIDI RECORDS has sent a message but has not received a submission.
- Next action: Wait for response or follow up once.
- Email/template to use: Direct message, email to a band, or follow-up template.
- Red flags: Pushy demands, unrealistic expectations, unclear communication.
- Owner notes:

## Submitted

- What it means: Artist has sent music and basic information.
- Next action: Confirm links work and log the submission.
- Email/template to use: `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` Submission Received.
- Red flags: Broken links, too many unfocused links, missing contact info.
- Owner notes:

## Under Review

- What it means: The artist is being evaluated internally.
- Next action: Complete review checklist and scoring system.
- Email/template to use: No email required unless review takes longer than expected.
- Red flags: Unclear rights, weak songs, suspicious stats, poor public presentation.
- Owner notes:

## Needs More Info

- What it means: The submission has potential but lacks required information.
- Next action: Request missing credits, release status, ownership, or goals.
- Email/template to use: `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` Request More Information.
- Red flags: Avoids rights questions, cannot explain credits/splits, vague answers.
- Owner notes:

## Selected For Call

- What it means: The artist is worth a direct conversation.
- Next action: Schedule call and prepare questions.
- Email/template to use: `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` Selected For A Call.
- Red flags: Missed call without explanation, demands guarantees, refuses written terms.
- Owner notes:

## Package Inquiry

- What it means: Artist may fit a paid artist development package.
- Next action: Define needs, scope, timeline, price, and payment status.
- Email/template to use: `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` Artist Development Package Inquiry.
- Red flags: Wants unpaid work, unclear budget, expects signing from paid services.
- Owner notes:

## Partnership Review

- What it means: Artist may fit a selected single release or deeper partnership.
- Next action: Review rights, assets, budget, release plan, and agreement needs.
- Email/template to use: Single Release Partnership Inquiry or Full Label Deal Inquiry.
- Red flags: Unclear ownership, no split sheet, no written agreement, unreleased samples.
- Owner notes:

## Agreement Pending

- What it means: Project is approved in principle but cannot start yet.
- Next action: Prepare or review written agreement and confirm payment if applicable.
- Email/template to use: Onboarding Next Steps or Payment / Manual Start Instructions.
- Red flags: Artist wants work to start before agreement, changes terms verbally.
- Owner notes:

## Active Project

- What it means: Agreement is in place and work has started.
- Next action: Complete onboarding, metadata, rollout, and task tracking.
- Email/template to use: Project-specific update emails.
- Red flags: Missed deadlines, missing assets, scope creep, new rights issues.
- Owner notes:

## Released

- What it means: The release is public or the agreed package work is complete.
- Next action: Save links, complete follow-up posts, review results, archive assets.
- Email/template to use: Release follow-up or thank-you note.
- Red flags: Incorrect metadata, broken links, late ownership disputes.
- Owner notes:

## Archived

- What it means: Artist or project is inactive, passed, completed, or paused indefinitely.
- Next action: Store notes and mark reason for archive.
- Email/template to use: Not a Fit or no-response closeout if needed.
- Red flags: Do not delete useful history unless required.
- Owner notes:
