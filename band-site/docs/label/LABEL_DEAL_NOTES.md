# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Label Deal Notes

These notes define internal working categories for KAMDRIDI RECORDS artist support and label relationships. They are meant to keep conversations clear before any agreement is drafted.

## A) Artist Development Package

Artist Development Package work is a paid creative service.

It is intended for artists who need practical support with branding, EPK development, release strategy, visual direction, and rollout planning.

Key points:

- Paid creative service
- No automatic label signing
- No royalty claim unless separately agreed in writing
- Scope should be defined before work starts
- Deliverables should be clear and realistic
- Payment status should be confirmed before work begins
- Best for artists who need structure, presentation, and release planning support

Possible deliverables:

- Brand direction notes
- EPK copy and asset checklist
- Release rollout plan
- Promo copy
- Social content direction
- Basic website/page direction

## B) Single Release Partnership

Single Release Partnership work is by selection only.

It applies to one selected single and should be limited in scope, term, and obligation. A written agreement is required before any release work begins.

Key points:

- By selection only
- One selected single
- Limited term
- Written agreement required
- Revenue split defined in writing
- Approved direct costs only
- No long-term lock-in unless separately agreed
- Rights, credits, splits, and ownership must be confirmed before release

Before approval:

- Confirm master ownership
- Confirm songwriter and producer credits
- Confirm splits
- Confirm artwork and visual direction
- Confirm distributor plan
- Confirm marketing responsibilities

## C) Full Label Deal

A Full Label Deal is by selection only and reserved for serious projects with strong identity and long-term potential.

It should apply to one EP or one album unless a separate written agreement says otherwise.

Key points:

- By selection only
- For serious projects with strong identity and long-term potential
- One EP or one album
- Clear term
- Defined revenue split
- Approved recoupable costs only
- Optional future project only if both sides agree
- Written agreement required before work begins
- Rights, credits, splits, ownership, budget, and release responsibilities must be clear

Before approval:

- Review full project quality
- Confirm artist reliability
- Confirm ownership and credits
- Confirm budget and recoupment rules
- Confirm release timeline
- Confirm exit or termination language

## Disclaimer

These are internal planning notes, not legal contracts. Final agreements must be reviewed before real signatures.
