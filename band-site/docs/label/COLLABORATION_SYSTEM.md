# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Collaboration System

Purpose: document cross-project collaboration strategy across KAM DRIDI, IRON COUNTY GHOSTS, June Marlowe, archive reworks, duets, featured artists, and future label projects.

## Collaboration Pillars

### KAM DRIDI Solo Projects

KAM DRIDI solo projects can carry personal writing, production, archive material, or direct artist identity. Keep credits clear when solo material overlaps with label or band projects.

### IRON COUNTY GHOSTS Band Projects

IRON COUNTY GHOSTS can operate as a band/project identity for selected songs, releases, visuals, and archive reinterpretations. Keep band credits separate from solo credits unless a collaboration is intentional.

### June Marlowe Future Solo Path

June Marlowe may have a future solo path. Keep any breakup, transition, or solo future plan internal only. Do not publish publicly yet.

### Archive Reworks

Archive Reworks can reinterpret older KAM DRIDI demos through current projects. Preserve original writing/source credits and clearly document new performance, arrangement, and production credits.

### Future Duets

Duets can be used when the song benefits from two distinct voices or story perspectives. Confirm public credit format before announcement.

### Featured Artist Strategy

Featured artists should be used when the feature adds real creative value. Do not add features only for appearance. Confirm rights, splits, public spelling, and promo expectations.

### Label Universe Rules

- Keep public credits accurate.
- Keep internal future plans private until approved.
- Do not confuse solo, band, archive, and feature credits.
- Do not create public relationship/story claims before they are ready.
- Use written approvals for featured artists and collaborators.
- Keep each release's ownership and credit notes in the release file.

## Future Credit Examples

- KAM DRIDI feat. June Marlowe
- KAM DRIDI feat. IRON COUNTY GHOSTS
- IRON COUNTY GHOSTS reimagines KAM DRIDI

## Internal Review Questions

- Which project identity should lead this song?
- Are the credits accurate?
- Is any story detail internal-only?
- Are splits and permissions documented?
- Does the collaboration make the song clearer or stronger?
