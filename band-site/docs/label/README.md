# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Label Documents

Index of internal label operation documents. These files are for planning, review, operations, and QA. They are not public site content.

## Documents

- `90_DAY_LABEL_ACTION_PLAN.md` - Practical 90-day plan for website readiness, announcements, outreach, submissions, campaigns, and review.
- `AGREEMENT_DRAFTS_NOT_LEGAL.md` - Non-legal outline drafts for artist development, single release partnership, and full label deal documents.
- `ARCHIVE_REWORKS_SYSTEM.md` - Internal system for reimagining older KAM DRIDI demos through label projects while preserving original credits.
- `ARTIST_CRM_PIPELINE.md` - Artist pipeline stages from lead discovery through active project, release, or archive.
- `ARTIST_SCORING_SYSTEM.md` - 100-point artist scoring model with thresholds and red flags.
- `ARTIST_SUBMISSION_TEMPLATE.md` - Intake template for artist submissions and required project information.
- `COLLABORATION_SYSTEM.md` - Internal strategy for solo, band, archive, duet, and featured-artist collaboration credits.
- `CONTENT_CALENDAR_TEMPLATE.md` - Reusable 30-day content calendar for release and social rollout planning.
- `EMAIL_TEMPLATES.md` - Professional email templates for submission replies, follow-ups, onboarding, and deal inquiries.
- `LABEL_DASHBOARD_SCHEMA.md` - Future dashboard schema for artists, releases, tracks, rights, campaigns, and sync.
- `LABEL_DEAL_NOTES.md` - Internal explanation of artist development packages, single release partnerships, and full label deals.
- `LABEL_OPERATING_MANUAL.md` - Simple operating manual covering mission, workflows, payments, documentation, and next 90 days.
- `ONBOARDING_CHECKLIST.md` - Checklist for selected artists before work starts.
- `OUTREACH_MESSAGES.md` - Outreach copy for artist submissions, social posts, DMs, and email.
- `PRESS_KIT_REQUIREMENTS.md` - Standard EPK requirements for artists, projects, releases, press, and licensing.
- `PROMO_POST_TEMPLATES.md` - Short social announcement templates for label and release promotion.
- `QA_CHECKLIST_FOR_CODEX1_SITE.md` - Manual checklist Kam can use to verify Codex 1 public site work.
- `RELEASE_METADATA_CHECKLIST.md` - Metadata, ownership, asset, distribution, and post-release checklist.
- `RELEASE_ROLLOUT_PLAN.md` - Reusable rollout timeline for singles, EPs, and albums.
- `REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md` - Internal checklist for evaluating submissions before making a decision.
- `SYNC_READINESS_CHECKLIST.md` - Checklist for preparing tracks for sync/licensing review.

## Importable Templates

- `templates/README.md` - Notes for using the importable CSV templates safely.
- `templates/artists.csv` - Artist/project records for CRM and roster-style tracking.
- `templates/releases.csv` - Release-level records for singles, EPs, albums, assets, and campaign status.
- `templates/tracks.csv` - Track metadata records for credits, files, tags, and sync prep.
- `templates/rights.csv` - Rights and clearance tracker with unknown fields marked for confirmation.
- `templates/campaigns.csv` - Campaign tracker for announcement, release day, newsletter, press, and website tasks.
- `templates/sync_catalog.csv` - Sync catalog tracker for clearance, versions, stems, tags, and licensing readiness.
- `templates/submissions.csv` - Submission intake tracker for artist review and next actions.
- `templates/tasks.csv` - Internal task tracker for label operations, releases, rights, and campaigns.

## Operating Rule

Keep these documents practical and current. Do not add fake roster artists, fake stats, legal certainty, or guaranteed results.
