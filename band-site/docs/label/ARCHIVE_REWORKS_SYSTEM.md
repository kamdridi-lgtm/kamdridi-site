# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Archive Reworks System

Purpose: document the KAMDRIDI RECORDS Archive Reworks concept for older KAM DRIDI demos reinterpreted through label artists and projects.

## Concept

KAMDRIDI RECORDS can reinterpret older KAM DRIDI demos through label artists, bands, and projects. The goal is to preserve the original writing/source history while creating new versions that fit current project identities.

Archive Reworks should be handled carefully. They are not new songs with erased history. They are new interpretations of older source material.

## Example

- Song: Our Lost Dreams
- Original demo: KAM DRIDI, 1993
- Reimagined by: IRON COUNTY GHOSTS
- Lead vocal: June Marlowe
- Series: KAMDRIDI RECORDS Archive Reworks

## Public Wording

"Originally demoed by KAM DRIDI in 1993. Reimagined by IRON COUNTY GHOSTS through the KAMDRIDI RECORDS Archive Reworks series."

## Internal Rules

- Do not miscredit songs.
- Always preserve original writing/source credits.
- Confirm which version is being released.
- Keep original demo notes separate from new release notes.
- Confirm new performance, production, and arrangement credits.
- Do not imply a song is brand new if it is based on archival material.
- Do not publish internal story details unless intentionally approved.
