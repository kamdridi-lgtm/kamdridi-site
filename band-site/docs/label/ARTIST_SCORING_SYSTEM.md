# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Artist Scoring System

Use this scoring system after the first review checklist is complete. Scores support decision-making; they do not replace judgment, rights review, or direct conversation.

## 100-Point Model

| Category | Points | What To Look For |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Songwriting / songs | 20 | Strong songs, clear hooks, emotional weight, repeat-listen value |
| Vocal identity / performance | 15 | Distinct voice, believable delivery, confidence, consistency |
| Production quality | 10 | Clean recording, mix quality, master quality, release readiness |
| Visual identity | 15 | Artwork, photos, typography, style consistency, recognizable direction |
| Artist story / positioning | 10 | Clear background, message, audience, and project identity |
| Work ethic / professionalism | 10 | Communication, organization, follow-through, realistic attitude |
| Market fit with KAMDRIDI RECORDS | 10 | Fit with label direction, brand, and practical ability to support |
| Release readiness | 5 | Metadata, assets, distributor status, rollout preparedness |
| Long-term potential | 5 | Growth potential beyond one song or one campaign |

## Decision Thresholds

- 0-49: Pass
- 50-64: Maybe / watchlist
- 65-79: Request more info or call
- 80-100: Strong candidate

## Red Flags

Any of these can override a high score:

- No clear songs
- Fake numbers
- Bad communication
- Unclear ownership
- No credits/splits
- Unrealistic expectations
- Expects guaranteed streams/playlists/income
- Refuses written agreement
