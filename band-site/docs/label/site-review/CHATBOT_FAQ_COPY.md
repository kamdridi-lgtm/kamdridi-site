# Label Assistant FAQ Copy

Use these as safe short answers for the KAMDRIDI RECORDS Label Assistant. Keep answers direct and avoid claims that are not confirmed.

## What is KAMDRIDI RECORDS?

KAMDRIDI RECORDS is a boutique label and artist development operation focused on serious artists, strong identity, release planning, and selected partnership review.

## How do I submit music?

Email 1-3 of your strongest song links, a short bio, social links, release status, and what support you need to kamdridi@proton.me.

## What packages are available?

KAMDRIDI RECORDS may offer artist development support, release planning, EPK help, visual direction, and rollout planning. Selected partnership reviews are separate from paid services.

## How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on scope. Paid work should be confirmed in writing before anything starts. Email kamdridi@proton.me with your goals and budget range.

## Can I buy a label deal?

No. Paid services do not buy a label deal. Label partnerships are by selection only and require written terms.

## What is the roster?

The current confirmed KAMDRIDI RECORDS project focus includes IRON COUNTY GHOSTS and KAM DRIDI-related releases. Public roster details should only show confirmed projects.

## What releases are available?

Confirmed releases should be listed on the releases page when real public links and details are available. Do not rely on unconfirmed metadata.

## Do you guarantee streams/playlists/income?

No. KAMDRIDI RECORDS does not guarantee streams, playlists, income, press, fan growth, or commercial results.

## Do you accept licensing inquiries?

Yes, licensing and sync inquiries can be sent to kamdridi@proton.me with usage details, deadline, territory, media type, and budget.

## How do I contact the label?

Email kamdridi@proton.me.

## What about Stripe?

Stripe may be used later for payments, but do not use fake checkout links. Payment terms must be confirmed in writing.

## Is IRON COUNTY GHOSTS AI-assisted?

IRON COUNTY GHOSTS is part of the KAMDRIDI creative and label ecosystem. Any public AI-assisted wording should be intentional, accurate, and approved before publishing.
