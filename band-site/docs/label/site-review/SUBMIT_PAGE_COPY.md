# Submit Page Copy

## Page Title

Submit Music to KAMDRIDI RECORDS

## Intro

KAMDRIDI RECORDS reviews selected artist submissions for development support, release planning, and possible label partnership. Send focused material and clear information so the project can be reviewed properly.

## What Artists Should Send

- Artist or band name
- Real name / contact person
- Location
- Genre
- 1-3 strongest song links
- Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, or private links if available
- Short bio
- Social links
- Current release status
- Upcoming release plans
- What kind of support you need
- Any budget or package interest
- Rights or credits notes if relevant

## Who Should Submit

Submit if you have serious songs, clear creative direction, and realistic expectations. KAMDRIDI RECORDS is a better fit for artists who are willing to communicate clearly, organize their materials, and work from written terms.

## What Happens Next

Submissions are reviewed internally. If there may be a fit, KAMDRIDI RECORDS may request more information, schedule a call, discuss a paid artist development package, or review a selected partnership path.

Not every submission will receive a detailed review or offer.

## No-Guarantee Disclaimer

KAMDRIDI RECORDS does not guarantee streams, playlist placement, press, income, signing, or commercial results.
