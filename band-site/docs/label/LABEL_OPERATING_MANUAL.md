# KAMDRIDI RECORDS Label Operating Manual

## 1. Mission

KAMDRIDI RECORDS exists to support serious artists with strong identity, clear music, and practical release goals. The label should operate with direct communication, written agreements, realistic expectations, and careful documentation.

## 2. What KAMDRIDI RECORDS Does

- Reviews artist submissions
- Offers selected artist development services
- Builds EPK, branding, release, and promo plans
- Supports selected single releases
- Reviews full label opportunities for serious projects
- Keeps internal documentation for each artist and release

## 3. What KAMDRIDI RECORDS Does Not Promise

- No guaranteed streams
- No guaranteed playlists
- No guaranteed income
- No guaranteed press
- No automatic signing after paid service work
- No royalty claim unless separately agreed in writing
- No work without clear scope and written agreement

## 5. Artist Development Packages

Artist Development Packages are paid creative services. They may include branding, EPK work, release planning, visual direction, rollout support, and practical artist presentation work.

Required before starting:

- Scope of work
- Price or payment terms
- Timeline
- Artist responsibilities
- Deliverables
- Revision limits
- Written agreement

## 6. Submission Workflow

1. Receive submission.
2. Confirm basic information is complete.
3. Check that links work.
4. Log contact details.
5. Review best 1-3 songs.
6. Request missing information if needed.
7. Move to review checklist or pass.

## 7. Review Workflow

1. Complete `REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`.
2. Score using `ARTIST_SCORING_SYSTEM.md`.
3. Check red flags.
4. Decide: pass, watchlist, request info, call, package, or partnership review.
5. Send the correct email template.
6. Store notes in the artist file.

## 8. Onboarding Workflow

1. Confirm selected path.
2. Confirm written agreement.
3. Confirm payment status if paid package.
4. Collect artist contact information.
5. Collect music, credits, splits, visuals, and bio.
6. Confirm timeline.
7. Complete `ONBOARDING_CHECKLIST.md`.
8. Start work only after required items are complete.

## 9. Release Workflow

1. Confirm rights, credits, and splits.
2. Confirm master and artwork.
3. Complete metadata checklist.
4. Upload to distributor.
5. Prepare EPK and promo copy.
6. Run rollout plan.
7. Capture links after release.
8. Update internal catalog notes.

## 10. Promo Workflow

1. Prepare announcement copy.
2. Prepare short video or visual assets.
3. Confirm links.
4. Post release-day content.
5. Follow up after one week.
6. Recap after 30 days.
7. Save useful feedback, press, and links.

## 11. Licensing/Sync Inquiries

Before discussing licensing or sync:

- Confirm master owner
- Confirm publishing owner
- Confirm writers and splits
- Confirm clean and instrumental versions if available
- Confirm who can approve licensing
- Do not quote legal terms without review

## 12. Payment Status / Stripe Later

Paid package work should have a clear payment status before work starts. Stripe or another payment system can be added later, but no fake checkout links should be used.

Status options:

- Not required
- Quote sent
- Invoice/manual payment pending
- Paid
- Partial payment
- Refunded
- On hold

## 13. Documentation Checklist
